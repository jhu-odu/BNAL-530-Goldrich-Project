Follow your team’s definitions

You do not need to use every column. Use the calculations, denominators and analysis populations specified in your template. Provider and tester counts count appointments, not distinct staff. Completion rate is Completed ÷ Scheduled, not capacity utilization.

The appointment and session datasets do not support a reliable patient-level join. If you use both, present complementary analyses rather than linking session totals to individual appointments.

Working with weather data

The weather dataset provides half-day average temperature (°F), session-window precipitation (inches) and half-day mean wind (mph), together with daily observations. Match half-day observations using both Date and Session block; match daily observations by Date. The clinic-session dataset also contains the prepared half-day weather measures. The measures use provisional local windows of 8 a.m.–noon for AM and 1–5 p.m. for PM; exact clinic hours are unavailable.

For Team 5, Hot conditions means average temperature strictly above 85°F, Rain conditions means precipitation of at least 0.10 inch, and Strong wind means mean wind of at least 20 mph. These are project definitions, not official storm warnings.

Weather observations: Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive of Norfolk Airport reports. Read your template’s Data Guide for measurement definitions and assumptions.
